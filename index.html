About Me

Hi, I'm Paul Ssekanaabi, widely known as Mr. Paul 🇺🇬, an ICT educator, technology enthusiast, and digital creator from Uganda with a passion for empowering people through technology. I currently serve as a Secondary School Teacher, where I teach Computer Studies, manage the school's ICT infrastructure, support teachers and students with technology, and develop digital solutions that improve learning and administration. Beyond the classroom, I enjoy building websites, designing graphics, livestreaming major events, creating educational content, and exploring emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, networking, and cybersecurity. I believe technology should solve real problems, simplify work, and create opportunities for everyone. Through Mr. Paul ICT Academy, my mission is to help students, teachers, and professionals gain practical digital skills that prepare them for today's technology-driven world. I believe learning is most effective when it is practical, engaging, and accessible. Whether I'm teaching, troubleshooting a network, designing a website, creating content, or mentoring future tech enthusiasts, my goal remains the same: To inspire, educate, and empower people through technology. Learn. Practice. Succeed.
